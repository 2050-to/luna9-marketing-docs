## The tools

### [Wix](https://www.wix.com/)

Wix fits a small business that wants bookings, payments, and a large app list in one place. Its AI gets you past a blank page. After that, the site lives on Wix and you edit it there. Wix does not give you the site to host somewhere else.

### [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/)

Squarespace fits a service business, a creator, or a small store that wants a polished template. AI sits on top of that system. After launch, you edit the site yourself. It is less free-form than Framer and less extensible than Wix.

### [Framer](https://www.framer.com/)

Framer fits a startup or a design-led marketing site. The canvas is the product, and AI helps you fill it. The site stays on Framer. It is a weaker fit when you need native bookings or a shop.

### [Durable](https://durable.com/)

Durable fits a solo or local service business that wants a site fast, plus a light CRM and invoicing. Generation is the start. There is no HTML export in its help center as of 13 October 2026, so leaving means rebuilding. After launch, you edit the site yourself.

### [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/)

Lovable fits someone who wants a prompt to become a web app, with auth and a database, and who will own the project. You can download the code on a paid plan or sync it to GitHub. Deploy and later changes are yours. Credits run out as you build.

### [Bolt](https://bolt.new/)

Bolt fits the same job as Lovable. A prompt becomes a project you can export as a zip or connect to GitHub. You deploy it. You maintain it.

### [v0](https://v0.app/)

v0 fits a developer who wants generated interface code and will deploy and maintain it. It is a coding tool, not a host that stays on the site.

## Questions

### What is an evolving site builder?

A builder that stays after launch. You say what should change. It plans the change, ships it, and keeps the site. A one-time generator hands you a draft and leaves.

### How is LunaNine different from Wix or Squarespace?

Wix and Squarespace host a site you edit yourself after the first generation. LunaNine keeps the conversation. You say the change. It ships the change. All three host the site. None of them hand you the source to leave with.

### How is LunaNine different from Lovable or Bolt?

Lovable and Bolt turn a prompt into a project you can take. You deploy it and you keep it up. LunaNine hosts the site and keeps changing it when you ask. You do not leave with the source.

### Do I keep editing the site myself?

With LunaNine, no. You say what should change. It does the change and keeps the site. With a hosted builder, you edit in their canvas. With a code agent, you edit the code.
