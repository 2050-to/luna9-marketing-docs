---
title: Getting started
description: Create an account and open chat.
---

Signup is open. There is no password.

1. Open [lunanineai.com/signup](https://lunanineai.com/signup).
2. Sign in with Google, a link we send to your email, or a wallet that holds $LUNA9.
3. You land on chat at `/app/chat`.

Chat needs a Starter or Base plan. Pay with a card in [Settings](https://lunanineai.com/app/settings/), or connect a wallet that unlocks the matching holder grant.

Already have an account? Use [lunanineai.com/login](https://lunanineai.com/login).
