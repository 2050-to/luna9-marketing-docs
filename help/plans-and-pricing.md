Pay with a card, or connect a wallet that holds $LUNA9. There is no metered billing.

Prices are on [Plans and pricing](https://luna9.space/pricing/). Checkout is in [Settings](https://luna9.space/app/settings/) after you sign in.

## Card plans

- **Base** - $25 / month or $250 / year. Text, voice notes, and live calls.
- **Pro** - $100 / month or $1,000 / year. Text, voice notes, and live calls.

Chat and calls need Base or Pro. Sign in first; checkout then opens Stripe.

There is no per-token invoice. Each plan includes a monthly allotment of conversations, background builds, voice notes, live calls, and generated images. When the allotment is used, wait for the window to roll, or move to Pro for a larger pool.

- **Base allotment** (rolling 30 days) - 600 conversations, 2 background builds, 40 voice notes (or 30 minutes), spoken replies up to 20,000 characters, 30 minutes of live calls, 25 generated images. Voice notes cap at 60 seconds.
- **Pro allotment** (rolling 30 days) - 1800 conversations, 6 background builds, 80 voice notes (or 60 minutes), spoken replies up to 60,000 characters, 90 minutes of live calls, 75 generated images. Voice notes cap at 120 seconds.

File storage sits outside that window. Base includes 500 MB of file storage. Pro includes 1.5 GB of file storage.

## $LUNA9 holders

Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain. Holding unlocks the matching grant.

- 1,000 $LUNA9 — 20 background checks per month (coming soon). The allotment is reserved; checks are not live yet.
- 200,000 $LUNA9 — Base plan.
- 1,000,000 $LUNA9 — Pro plan.

A wallet with fewer than 1,000 $LUNA9 can still sign in. The Stripe paywall then applies, same as an unpaid Google account.
