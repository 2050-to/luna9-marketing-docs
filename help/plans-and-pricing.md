One flat price. You say the site you need. LunaNine plans it, builds it, ships it, and maintains it. Pay with a card, or connect a wallet that holds $LUNA9.

Prices are on [Plans and pricing](https://lunanineai.com/pricing/). Checkout is in [Settings](https://lunanineai.com/app/settings/) after you sign in.

## Card plans

- **Starter** - $20 / month or $200 / year. Enough to run the work on a few projects.
- **Base** - $30 / month or $300 / year. Enough to run the work in a message.

Chat is on Starter and Base. Sign in, then choose a plan in Settings.

No meters and no surprise bills. Each plan is a month of that work. When the month runs out, it refills.

**Included with Starter**

- Build
- Host
- Process Payments - 8% fee (5% platform + 3% Stripe)
- Generate Images
- Modify and Maintain
- Email Waitlists
- Projects - up to 3
- Bring Your Own Domain - up to 1

**Included with Base**

- Build
- Host
- Process Payments - 5% fee (2% platform + 3% Stripe)
- Generate Images
- Modify and Maintain
- Email Waitlists
- Projects - up to 5
- Bring Your Own Domain - up to 3
- Sales
- Background checks

**Starter, each month**

The monthly room matches Base.

**Base, each month**

- 600 conversations to shape the work
- 2 builds that finish while you are away
- 25 pictures made for you
- 500 MB to keep your files

## Hold $LUNA9

Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain. The balance you hold includes the matching plan.

- 1,000 $LUNA9 - 20 checks a month, coming soon. That room is set aside. Checks are not available yet.
- $30 of $LUNA9 - Base included.

Under 1,000 $LUNA9, you can still sign in and pay by card.
