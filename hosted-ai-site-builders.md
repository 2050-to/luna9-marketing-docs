# Hosted AI site builders

A hosted AI site builder generates the first site and keeps it on its own host. You do not take the source with you. After that first build, you edit the site yourself.

Wix, Squarespace, Framer, and Durable are hosted AI site builders. Lovable, Bolt, and v0 are not. They hand you a project you deploy. LunaNine hosts the site, and it keeps changing the site when you say so.

## What hosted means

### The site stays on their host

The site lives where they host it. You do not move it to a server of your own.

### You do not leave with the source

There is no project to download and host somewhere else.

### After launch, you edit it

The first build is generated. Later changes are yours, in their canvas.

## The hosted ones

Checked October 2026.

### [Wix](https://www.wix.com/)

Wix generates a site, then keeps it on Wix. Bookings, payments, and apps live there. After the first build, you edit the site yourself. Wix does not give you the site to host somewhere else.

### [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/)

Squarespace generates from a polished template and keeps the site on Squarespace. After launch, you edit it yourself. It is less free-form than Framer and less extensible than Wix.

### [Framer](https://www.framer.com/)

Framer keeps a design-led site on Framer. AI helps you fill the canvas. You edit it there after launch. It is a weaker fit when you need native bookings or a shop.

### [Durable](https://durable.com/)

Durable generates a service site quickly and keeps it on Durable, with a light CRM and invoicing. There is no HTML export in its help center as of 13 October 2026, so leaving means rebuilding. After launch, you edit the site yourself.

## Outside this group

Lovable, Bolt, and v0 turn a prompt into a project you deploy and maintain. They are code agents, not hosted AI site builders. See the [Evolving site builder review](https://lunanineai.com/reviews/evolving-site-builder).
