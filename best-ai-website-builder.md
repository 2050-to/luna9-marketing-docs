## Questions

### Which AI website builder is best?

The one whose limit matches the job. Wix for a shop, bookings, and apps. Squarespace, or Framer if you will design it, when the look is the point. 10Web when you want a WordPress site you can move. LunaNine when you say the next change and want it shipped.

### Which AI website builder is cheapest to start?

Hostinger advertises the lowest start, and that rate needs a long prepay. Read the renewal before you compare it with a monthly plan.

### Which AI website builder lets me leave with the site?

Lovable lets you take the project. 10Web builds on WordPress, so leaving is a migration. Wix, Squarespace, Framer, Durable, Hostinger, and LunaNine host the site. You do not take LunaNine's source with you.
